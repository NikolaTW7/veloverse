How It Works Together

Bicycle components work as connected systems. In the drivetrain, the rider's effort passes through several parts before it moves the bicycle forward.

  1. 1PedallingThe rider pushes the pedals
  2. 2CranksetThe cranks turn the chainring
  3. 3ChainThe chain carries the power backwards
  4. 4CassetteThe chosen sprocket sets the gear
  5. 5Rear WheelThe wheel turns and the bicycle moves
