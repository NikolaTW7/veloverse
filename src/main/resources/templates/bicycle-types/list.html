Understanding the Differences

Choosing the right bicycle type depends on several factors that shape the riding experience.

Terrain

Smooth tarmac and rough dirt trails need very different tyres and frames.

Riding Position

Low, forward positions favour speed, while upright positions favour comfort and visibility.

Speed

Weight, tyre width and rolling resistance affect how much effort it takes to keep moving.

Comfort

Wider tyres and suspension help absorb road vibration and trail impacts.

Durability

Frames and parts are built differently depending on how hard the bike will be used.

Intended Use

Commuting, racing, touring or casual riding: every type is designed with a purpose.
