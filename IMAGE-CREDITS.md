## Rules for adding a photograph later

1. Use only a photo whose own page shows a free licence (Unsplash License, Pexels License,
   Magnific free with attribution, or a Creative Commons licence on Wikimedia Commons that allows
   commercial use and changes, such as CC BY or CC BY-SA). Never Unsplash+ or premium content.
2. Look at the full image: no prominent logos, no identifiable people, and it really shows the
   intended subject.
3. Never use a generic bicycle photo for a specific real model, and never use a photo of one brand's bicycle for another brand.
4. Keep the original outside the project. Save WebP versions at 640 and 1280 px wide in
   `src/main/resources/static/images/photos/`.
5. Add the photo to `templates/fragments/photos.html` and add a row to the matching table above.
   Creative Commons photos use `licensedPhoto`, so the author, licence and changes are shown.
